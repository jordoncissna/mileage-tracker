Privacy Policy

Milo Mileage Tracker · Effective July 31, 2026 · Operated by Ridgeline Management Group LLC
The short version: Milo stores the trip records you create so you can access them across your devices and produce tax reports. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't track you around the web. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and a securely hashed password, used only to sign you in and send account emails (verification, password reset).
  • Trip records you enter. Dates, mileage, origin and destination addresses, business purpose, and category. These exist because you typed them — Milo does not track your location in the background.
  • Device settings. Preferences such as mileage rates, saved home/office addresses, and display options are stored locally in your browser (localStorage), not on our servers.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, run third-party analytics trackers, or use advertising cookies.

How we use your information

  • To sync your trip log across your devices and keep it available when you sign in.
  • To compute deduction values and generate the reports and exports you request (CSV, PDF).
  • To send essential account emails — verification, password reset, and welcome messages. We do not send marketing email.

Service providers we rely on

  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication. Your trip records are stored in Supabase (Postgres) with row-level security, so each account can only read its own data.
  • Google Maps Platform — when you use address autocomplete or distance calculation, the addresses you enter are sent to Google to compute the route. Google's handling of that request is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Resend — delivers our account emails (verification, password reset).

These providers process data on our behalf to run the service. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Data retention & deletion

  • Your trip records are kept until you delete them. You can delete individual trips or use Clear all in the app at any time.
  • To delete your entire account and all associated data, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.
  • You can export a complete copy of your data at any time (CSV export or the tax-ready PDF report).

Security

All traffic between your device and our servers is encrypted with TLS. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Database access is protected by row-level security so your records are isolated to your account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. Milo gives you all of these directly in the app; for anything you can't do yourself, email us and we will help.

Children

Milo is a business-expense tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and note the change in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
